# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers
pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insulting, derogatory or personal/political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (such as a physical address or email) without
  permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and contributions that do not follow
this Code of Conduct, and will explain the moderation decision when needed.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an
individual represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **enraigaming@yahoo.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated fairly, and the reporter's privacy is protected. Maintainers who do not enforce this
Code of Conduct in good faith may face consequences as determined by other maintainers.
